# What is Physical AI?

Physical AI represents a paradigm shift from traditional software-based artificial intelligence to intelligence that is embodied and interacts with the physical world. Unlike conventional AI systems that operate primarily in digital domains, Physical AI integrates computational intelligence with physical systems, enabling machines to perceive, reason, and act in real-world environments.

## Core Principles

Physical AI combines three fundamental components:

1. **Embodiment**: The AI system has a physical form that interacts with the environment
2. **Real-time Interaction**: The system processes sensory inputs and generates actions in real-time
3. **Physical Reasoning**: The AI understands and operates within the constraints of physical laws

## Applications and Impact

Physical AI systems are transforming industries by bridging the gap between digital intelligence and physical tasks. From autonomous vehicles to robotic assistants, these systems are becoming integral to modern life.

## Challenges and Opportunities

The development of Physical AI presents unique challenges including sensor fusion, real-time processing, safety considerations, and the integration of multiple physical systems. However, these challenges also present significant opportunities for innovation and advancement in robotics and AI.
