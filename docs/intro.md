# Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to **Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics**, a comprehensive textbook covering the intersection of artificial intelligence and embodied systems.

This textbook explores the fundamental principles, technologies, and applications of Physical AI - intelligence that is embodied and interacts with the physical world. From foundational concepts to advanced applications, you'll discover how humanoid robots are transforming industries and society.

## About This Textbook

This comprehensive guide is structured into seven parts:

- **Part 1**: Foundations of Physical AI - Core concepts and principles
- **Part 2**: Humanoid Robotics Fundamentals - Mechanical and control systems
- **Part 3**: Intelligence in the Body - Perception and learning
- **Part 4**: Physical AI Systems - Technologies and platforms
- **Part 5**: Applications - Real-world implementations
- **Part 6**: Ethics and Governance - Safety and societal considerations
- **Part 7**: Capstone and Future - Design and future directions

## Getting Started

This textbook is designed to provide both theoretical foundations and practical insights into Physical AI and humanoid robotics. Each section builds upon previous concepts while maintaining standalone accessibility.

The content covers cutting-edge research, practical implementation details, and ethical considerations essential for understanding this rapidly evolving field.
